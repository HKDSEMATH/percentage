Percentage Formulas

  • Simple interest: \(I = P \times r\% \times t\)
  • Simple amount: \(A = P(1 + r\% \cdot t)\)
  • Compound interest: \(A = P(1 + r\%)^n\)
  • Growth / depreciation: \(\text{New} = \text{Old}(1 \pm p\%)^n\)
  • Selling price: \(S = M \times (1 - d\%)\)
  • Profit / Loss %: \(\dfrac{S-C}{C}\times 100\%\)
  • Percentage change: \(\dfrac{\text{New}-\text{Old}}{\text{Old}}\times 100\%\)
  • Successive change: \((1+a\%)(1-b\%)\)
